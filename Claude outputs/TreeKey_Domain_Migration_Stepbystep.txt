TREEKEY.CO.UK MIGRATION - FULL STEP BY STEP
(you've bought the domain - this is everything from here to "the business is live on
treekey.co.uk and treekey.uk is a clean cold-email-only domain," in the order to do it)

Read this once end to end before starting - the order matters. Doing things out of
order (e.g. flipping the live website over before Resend/DNS are ready) is what causes
a few hours of broken emails or a dead site. Each phase says who does it: things marked
(YOU) happen in a dashboard I can't click into myself; things marked (ME) are code
edits I'll make in your files once you say go.

==============================================================
PHASE 1 - GET THE NEW DOMAIN ONTO CLOUDFLARE (YOU)
==============================================================
Why first: everything else (hosting, email, SSL) needs the domain's DNS to actually
be manageable before you can configure any of it.

1. In the Cloudflare dashboard, go to your account and add treekey.co.uk as a new site
   (Websites / Add a site). If you bought it through Cloudflare Registrar it may
   already be sitting in your account - check the Domain Registration page first.
2. If it's not already using Cloudflare's nameservers, Cloudflare will show you two
   nameserver addresses to set at the registrar. If you bought it through Cloudflare
   itself, this step is done automatically.
3. Wait for the zone to go "Active" in Cloudflare (usually fast, can take a few hours
   for a domain bought elsewhere).

Don't touch treekey.uk's DNS in this phase - leave it exactly as it is.

==============================================================
PHASE 2 - HOSTING: POINT RENDER AT THE NEW DOMAIN (YOU, then ME)
==============================================================
Why now: you want the new domain fully working and verified BEFORE you flip the
"official" site over to it, so there's no dead-site gap.

1. (YOU) In the Render dashboard, open your web service -> Settings -> Custom Domains
   -> Add Custom Domain -> enter treekey.co.uk (and www.treekey.co.uk if you want the
   www version to work too).
2. (YOU) Render will show you the exact DNS record(s) to add (usually a CNAME, or an
   A record for the root domain). Add those in Cloudflare's DNS tab for treekey.co.uk.
   One gotcha from Render's own docs: remove any AAAA records on the domain while
   you're doing this, they can conflict with verification.
3. (YOU) Back in Render, click Verify. Once it succeeds, Render automatically issues
   an SSL certificate for the domain (via Let's Encrypt) - no separate step needed.
   Can take a few minutes for DNS to propagate; if Verify fails immediately, wait 10
   minutes and try again before assuming something's wrong.
4. (YOU) Load https://treekey.co.uk directly in a browser once it's issued - you
   should see the exact same TreeKey site as treekey.uk (same app, just a second
   domain pointing at it). This is expected and fine, it's not "live" as the official
   domain until the next step.
5. STOP HERE and don't do step 5 until Resend (Phase 3) is fully verified, or every
   email the site sends will start failing.
5. (YOU) Once Phase 3 is done: change the PUBLIC_APP_URL environment variable on
   Render from https://treekey.uk to https://treekey.co.uk, and redeploy. This one
   setting is what actually makes treekey.co.uk "the real site" - it drives the
   sitemap, robots.txt, every login/checkout/unsubscribe link, and most of the email
   content.

==============================================================
PHASE 3 - EMAIL SENDING: VERIFY THE NEW DOMAIN ON RESEND (YOU)
==============================================================
Why before Phase 2 step 5: the moment PUBLIC_APP_URL points at the new domain, the
app will try to send real customer emails "from" it - so the domain needs to be
verified and ready first, or those emails will fail outright (HTTP 403 from Resend).

1. In the Resend dashboard, go to Domains -> Add Domain.
2. Resend's own recommendation is to use a subdomain for sending (e.g.
   notifications.treekey.co.uk or mail.treekey.co.uk) rather than the bare root
   domain - it's considered better deliverability practice and keeps sending
   infrastructure separate from the website itself. Worth doing this time even
   though treekey.uk currently sends from the bare domain.
3. Pick the region closest to your recipients (UK-based, so pick accordingly).
4. Resend will show you a Records tab with the exact DNS entries to add (TXT records
   for SPF/DKIM, possibly an MX record). Copy every one of these into Cloudflare's
   DNS tab for treekey.co.uk exactly as shown - don't paraphrase them.
5. Wait for verification - Resend says it's often within 15 minutes, but DNS can take
   up to 72 hours worst case. Don't move to Phase 2 step 5 until this shows Verified.
6. Once verified, add a DMARC record too (Resend prompts for this after verification).
   Worth getting this right from day one on the new domain rather than leaving it at
   p=none the way treekey.uk currently is.

==============================================================
Do these against treekey.co.uk after Phase 2 step 5 and Phase 4 are both done:

==============================================================
1. Fix DMARC on treekey.uk itself (p=none -> enforcing) - already flagged, now more
   important since this domain's only job from here is cold outreach.
2. Leave nick@treekey.uk / nickms@treekey.uk (the Instantly warmup mailboxes)

   they're doing now.
3. If you go ahead with the private staging/admin site idea we discussed on

   when you're testing on it (either stub the email sending in that environment, or

3. Once Phase 2 step 5 is live, treekey.uk's 301 redirect (set up back in Phase 2 if
   you want one) should already be sending any old visitors/bookmarks on to
   treekey.co.uk automatically.
