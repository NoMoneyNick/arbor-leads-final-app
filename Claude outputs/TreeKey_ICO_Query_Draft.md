# Draft query for ICO's free small-business advice service

*For the phone/online advice service at ico.org.uk (not a formal complaint or registration) — Data Protection guidance for small organisations. Read before sending: this is drafted, not sent. ICO's free advice is general guidance, not a substitute for the solicitor review in the evidence pack — worth doing both, in either order, but don't treat ICO's answer as legal sign-off.*

---

**Who we are:** A UK sole-trader business (pre-Ltd) that scans public council planning application registers for tree-work applications, and sells access to those applications (including the applicant's name and address, once purchased) to tree surgery contractors. Each application is only sold to one buyer.

**What I need to know:**

1. We collect the applicant's name and full address from public planning applications — this wasn't collected from the person directly. Are we required to proactively notify each applicant that we've done this and what it's used for, and if so, within what timeframe and by what method? Does linking to a privacy policy on our own website satisfy this, or does it need to reach the person directly?

2. Is there a minimum objection/opt-out mechanism we're required to provide, beyond stating the right to object in a privacy policy — and does it need to work even after the applicant's own request removes the record, so we don't re-collect the same person's data on a future application?

3. We separately hold a small number of records for planning applications that turned out not to be tree-related (e.g. HMO applications) — we have no commercial use for these. Is there a data minimisation obligation to delete these promptly rather than let them expire on a general retention schedule?

**What we've already done:** stopped short of full data minimisation on point 3, use a 56-day retention window for unsold records with no current expiry for sold records, and currently rely only on a privacy policy page (not proactive contact) for notice.
