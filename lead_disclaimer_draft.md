# Lead Accuracy Disclaimer & Limitation of Liability — Draft for Solicitor Review

*This is a first draft to hand to a solicitor before publishing anywhere on treekey.uk. It is written to reflect how the product actually works (sourced from public council planning data, automated classification, no manual verification of every lead) so the legal language matches reality rather than overpromising.*

## 1. Nature of the Data

Leads listed on Tree Key are derived from publicly available UK council planning application records and related public sources. Tree Key aggregates, filters, and classifies this information using automated processes, including automated assessment of whether an agent or representative named on an application appears to be a tree surgery or arboricultural business.

This classification is a best-effort estimate based on available information at the time of listing. It is not verified by a person for every lead, and it may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect.

## 2. No Guarantee of Availability or Current Status

A lead's status (including whether a tree surgeon or other contractor has already been engaged) reflects information available to Tree Key at the time the lead was discovered or last checked. Tree Key does not guarantee that:

- the underlying planning application is still active or undetermined;
- no contractor has since been engaged by the applicant, whether or not this is reflected in the public record;
- the contact or applicant information is current or accurate;
- the lead will result in a successful quote, contract, or completed job.

Users are responsible for conducting their own due diligence — including directly confirming with the applicant whether the work is still available — before committing time or resources to a lead.

## 3. No Guarantee of Business Outcome

Tree Key provides access to lead information only. Tree Key is not a party to, and has no involvement in, any subsequent contract, quote, or work arrangement between a user and a third party. Tree Key does not guarantee any level of lead volume, lead quality, conversion rate, or business outcome from use of the service.

## 4. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law:

- Tree Key's total liability arising out of or in connection with any lead, or the use of the service, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, is limited to the amount actually paid by the user for the specific lead(s) giving rise to the claim.
- Tree Key shall not be liable for any indirect, consequential, or special losses, including loss of profit, loss of business opportunity, or wasted expenditure (including quoting or travel costs), arising from reliance on a lead that proves to be inaccurate, outdated, or already actioned.

Nothing in this disclaimer excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or any other liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under English law.

## 5. Acknowledgement

By purchasing or accessing a lead, the user acknowledges that lead information is provided on an "as available" basis, sourced substantially from automated processing of public records, and agrees to conduct independent verification before relying on it commercially.
