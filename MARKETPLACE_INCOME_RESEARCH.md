# Autonomous income with low buyer commitment

Research checked 13 September 2026. This replaces the website-maintenance recommendation, which the user rejected.

## Decision

The best-fitting direction is a small product that customers can inspect or test before buying, delivered through an established marketplace. This reduces dependence on testimonials and business relationships. It does not remove competition, discovery, or the need to demonstrate quality.

Two closest candidates are original printable party games and paid cloud automations on Apify. A third, a preview-before-payment event-print generator, fits automation well but has a weaker route to inexpensive customers. None has sufficient evidence to promise consistent income within two weeks. These are explicitly closest candidates, not ideas certified to meet every original requirement.

The budget is no new monthly software subscription. Google Workspace and existing development subscriptions are available. Transaction charges, listing fees and computing deducted from sales are separate costs; no spending has been authorized. No platform accounts were opened, products published or customers contacted during this research.

## 1. Original printable party-game packs

Concrete product: a complete, original print-at-home birthday mystery for children aged 8–12, with ten puzzles, a host guide, hints, answers and an ink-saving version. Subsequent products could cover other ages or occasions. Begin with finished downloads, not individually written customer commissions.

The buyer sees the theme, page quality, preparation requirements and a sample puzzle before purchase. This is a small discretionary purchase rather than handing a supplier access to business systems. Reviews can help, but a demonstrable product offers a route to a first sale without prior client testimonials. This is a reasoned assessment, not a measured conversion result.

Claude Code could build the layout and puzzle-generation software using free libraries. Software can verify mathematical answers, unique solutions where applicable, print margins and matching answer keys. A person must still check whether the story and puzzles are understandable, enjoyable and age-appropriate. That initial creative testing cannot honestly be replaced by an AI score.

Finished files can be delivered automatically. Etsy explicitly supports instant digital downloads and requires the items to be made or designed by the seller. Its made-to-order workflow is different and should not be described as automatically fulfilled. [Etsy digital listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015628347-How-to-Manage-Your-Digital-Listings)

Competition is real: BrainMazeGames lists a ten-puzzle birthday escape-room download at €5.97. This establishes an actual competing product and asking price, not independently audited sales or profitability. Its advertised review count was not independently verified. [Competing birthday game](https://brainmazegames.com/product/e26-girls-10th-birthday-escape-room/)

Etsy reported 86.5 million active buyers at the end of 2025. This establishes substantial platform reach, not demand for our particular game or the likelihood of ranking in search. [Etsy annual results](https://investors.etsy.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/218/etsy-inc-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results)

An initial £8–£12 asking-price test is a proposed experiment, not a researched optimal price. If each sale eventually contributed £6 after all variable expenses, 50 monthly sales would contribute £300 before owner time and tax. Neither that margin nor that sales volume has been measured. Paid advertising could substantially reduce or eliminate it.

Adversarial assessment: free puzzles and cheap competing packs constrain price; a pretty PDF may be a poor game; marketplace discovery is uncertain; refunds and printing questions still require occasional attention. Use one tested game, a genuinely useful sample and clear instructions rather than producing hundreds of superficial listings. Those changes address quality and support risk, but do not prove customer acquisition.

There is no required monthly software subscription in this design, but Etsy charges listing and selling fees. A shop setup charge may also apply. New sellers' funds generally become eligible for deposit 14 days after a sale, sometimes longer. Therefore a first sale and cash in the bank are distinct milestones. [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360035902374-Etsy-Fee-Basics), [deposit timing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002080688-Why-Hasn-t-My-Money-Been-Deposited-Yet)

Verdict: strongest consumer-product fit; no proven two-week income outcome.

## 2. Sell executions of a small automation through Apify

Concrete mechanism: package a narrow, useful part of the existing crawler or processing code as an Apify Actor. Customers supply inputs, inspect sample results and pay for usage. The platform supplies execution infrastructure and billing. Apify explicitly says monetization is available on a free account. Development can use Python or JavaScript; using AI to build the program need not mean paying for AI inference every time it runs. [Apify publishing guide](https://blog.apify.com/how-to-monetize-api/)

This is a better use of existing engines than building another full marketplace. However, the engines' stated AI review scores do not establish that a particular tool works, is economical or has customers. The exact module remains unselected pending evidence of useful output and paid demand.

A candidate considered was a Shopify catalogue and price-change exporter. The competitor search found an exact price-monitoring Actor listed at $5 per 1,000 products. The fetched page showed two total users and zero monthly active users. These are a dated public snapshot, not live audited usage. This candidate cannot be described as commercially validated simply because it is listed. [Competing Shopify monitor](https://apify.com/technicaldost/shopify-price-delta-monitor)

Current Apify documentation calculates creator profit as 80% of eligible revenue minus platform running costs. Ordinary free-plan users do not generate creator revenue under that calculation. Thus total users, free runs and downloads must not be substituted for paying demand. [Pricing and costs](https://docs.apify.com/actors/publishing/monetize/pricing-and-costs)

For illustration in the platform's billing currency: $500 of eligible revenue and $50 of running costs would leave $350 before other expenses and tax. This is arithmetic, not a forecast, and it is not £350. Expensive proxies, retries or external APIs can destroy the assumed margin.

Payouts are processed after each calendar month, subject to verification and minimums of $20 for PayPal or $100 for other payout options. Creators have ongoing maintenance responsibilities. This is largely automated software delivery, not permanent unattended income. [Publishing terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions)

Adversarial assessment: marketplace exposure does not guarantee discovery; users may all be free; easy scrapers attract substitutes; target websites change; platform dependency remains. Before accepting this route, measure costs on realistic inputs and identify an existing task where paying users have a specific unresolved problem. Do not reuse TreeKey's exclusive lead inventory or customer data as public merchandise.

Verdict: strongest match to existing technical assets and free cloud deployment. No exact tool has yet passed the paid-demand test, and monthly payout timing does not satisfy a strict week-one cash requirement.

## 3. A preview-before-payment event-print generator

Concrete product: upload guest names and table assignments, generate matching seating charts and place cards, inspect the finished previews, then buy printable files. Deterministic layout code can handle this without runtime AI costs.

The competitor check found almost this exact model: WedSeatly previews a wedding chart before payment and charges £4.99 for its PDF pack. This is direct competition, not a unique idea. Its sales and profitability have not been established. [WedSeatly](https://www.wedseatly.com/)

The trust problem is reduced because the purchaser sees the actual output. The acquisition problem remains: a standalone site has to find visitors. Bundling genuinely useful outputs might support a higher price, but cannot be assumed to create demand.

Advertising stress test: at a hypothetical 50p per click and 3% purchase conversion, acquisition costs £16.67 per sale. That is already more than the revenue from a £9.99 product, before processing or refunds. A low ticket price is therefore not automatically easy money. Those click and conversion figures are illustrative, not researched market averages.

Verdict: strong fulfillment automation and demonstrability, weaker near-term distribution. Below the other two routes until a credible source of inexpensive visitors is identified.

## Other mechanisms screened

These are structural assessments, not exhaustive audits of every provider:

| Route | Why it falls below the finalists for this brief |
|---|---|
| Print-on-demand physical goods | Production, delivery, returns and acquisition consume margin; no demonstrated demand advantage here. |
| Affiliate sites and advertising-funded content | Automated publishing does not itself supply an audience or purchase intent. |
| Buying and reselling products | Can earn money, but sourcing capital, fulfillment and disputes weaken the free and autonomous fit. |
| Selling custom work or collecting bounties | Acceptance, delivery and payment require continuing project work; repeat income is not automatic. |
| Renting owned equipment or space | Potentially useful only if suitable assets already exist; those assets have not been established. |
| Royalties from books or similar catalogues | Automated fulfillment is possible, but discovery and payout delay remain. KDP, for example, generally pays about 60 days after the sales month ends. [KDP payments](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202187870) |

## What has and has not passed

We have established actual competing offerings, feasible automated fulfillment and routes without required new monthly software subscriptions. We have not established a reliable first-two-week acquisition channel, typical new-seller profit, or guaranteed availability of payouts in that period.

For a consumer direction, the printable party-game shop is the first bounded experiment. For reuse of existing software, Apify is the first distribution channel to assess. They should not both be built as large businesses before observing demand. In either case, a single useful product and actual paid usage are stronger evidence than a catalogue of generated ideas or platform-wide revenue statistics.
